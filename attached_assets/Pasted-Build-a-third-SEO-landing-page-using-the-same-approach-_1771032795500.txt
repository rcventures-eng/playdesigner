Section 6: FAQ (ID: faq)
H2: Frequently Asked Questions About the Play Library
Q1: Is the football play library free?
A1: Yes. Saving plays, tagging, cloning, and sharing are all free. The global library of pre-made community plays is available to premium users.
Q2: Do my saved plays carry over between seasons?
A2: Yes. Your play library is permanent. Plays you saved last season, last year, or three years ago are still in your library. Nothing gets deleted unless you remove it.
Q3: What's the difference between the play library and a playbook?
A3: The play library is your full collection of every play you've ever saved -- think of it as your filing cabinet. A playbook is a curated, ordered set of plays for a specific team or gameplan. You pull plays from your library into a playbook when you're building a gameplan.
Q4: What is the global library?
A4: The global library is a shared collection of plays created by coaches in the RC Football community. Premium users can browse it by formation, concept, or situation and copy any play into their personal library.
Q5: Can I share plays with players, not just coaches?
A5: Yes. Shared plays can be viewed by anyone you send them to. Players can open them on their phone or laptop for pre-game review. They see the diagram, the route labels, and any notes you've added.
Q6: How does cloning work?
A6: Cloning duplicates a play into a new, independent copy. You can change anything on the clone -- routes, positions, labels, formation -- without affecting the original. Coaches use this to create variations of a base play without starting from scratch each time.
