Section 6: FAQ (ID: faq)
H2: Frequently Asked Questions About the Playbook Maker
Q1: Is the football playbook maker free?
A1: Yes. Creating playbooks, importing rosters, adding plays, and organizing your gameplan are all free. Google Drive export is also included at no cost.
Q2: Can I import my roster from a spreadsheet?
A2: Yes. You can paste player names directly from a spreadsheet like Excel or Google Sheets. The import takes two clicks. You can also type names in manually if you prefer.
Q3: What are the color schemas for players under 12?
A3: The color schemas are simplified visual systems that replace complex terminology with color-coded assignments. Players under 12 can identify their role on a play by color rather than reading route names or blocking calls. It makes practice faster and cuts down on confusion.
Q4: Can I export my playbook to Google Drive?
A4: Yes. You can export to Google Docs (text-forward format) or Google Slides (one play per slide, visual layout). The export goes directly to your Google Drive so your coaching staff can access it right away.
Q5: Can I manage playbooks for more than one team?
A5: Yes. You can create and manage separate playbooks for multiple teams from a single account. Each playbook has its own roster, play order, and export options. There is no limit on the number of playbooks you can create.
Q6: Do my plays from the play designer carry over into the playbook?
A6: Yes. Any play you design and save in the RC Football play designer is available to add to any of your playbooks. The two tools are connected -- design in one, organize in the other.
