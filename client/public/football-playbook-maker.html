How the Playbook Maker Works

  1. Create a Playbook

    Hit "New Playbook" and give it a name -- "Varsity Offense Week 3" or "Flag U10 Season Playbook." Each playbook is its own container. You can have as many as you need.

  2. Import Your Roster

    Paste your player list from a spreadsheet or type names manually. Two clicks and your roster is loaded. The tool suggests offensive and defensive positions based on common alignments so you're not starting from zero.

  3. Add and Order Your Plays

    Pull plays from your play library into the playbook. Drag them into the order that matches your gameplan. Openers at the top, situational plays grouped together, red zone at the end -- whatever makes sense for how you call games.

    Export the finished playbook to Google Docs or Google Slides with one click. Hand it to your assistants, print copies for the press box, or pull it up on your tablet during the game.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Playbook Maker

Is the football playbook maker free?

Yes. Creating playbooks, importing rosters, adding plays, and organizing your gameplan are all free. Google Drive export is also included at no cost.

Can I import my roster from a spreadsheet?

Yes. You can paste player names directly from a spreadsheet like Excel or Google Sheets. The import takes two clicks. You can also type names in manually if you prefer.

What are the color schemas for players under 12?

The color schemas are simplified visual systems that replace complex terminology with color-coded assignments. Players under 12 can identify their role on a play by color rather than reading route names or blocking calls. It makes practice faster and cuts down on confusion.

Can I export my playbook to Google Drive?

Yes. You can export to Google Docs (text-forward format) or Google Slides (one play per slide, visual layout). The export goes directly to your Google Drive so your coaching staff can access it right away.

Can I manage playbooks for more than one team?

Yes. You can create and manage separate playbooks for multiple teams from a single account. Each playbook has its own roster, play order, and export options. There is no limit on the number of playbooks you can create.

Do my plays from the play designer carry over into the playbook?

Yes. Any play you design and save in the RC Football play designer is available to add to any of your playbooks. The two tools are connected -- design in one, organize in the other.
