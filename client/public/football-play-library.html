The Football Play Library That Keeps Every Play Within Reach

Designing plays is one thing. Finding them again when you need them is another.

RC Football's play library stores every play you create and keeps it organized. Tag plays by situation -- 3rd and long, goal line, two-minute drill -- and by concept -- screen pass, play-action, zone run. When Friday night comes and you need that specific red zone play you drew up in August, it's right where you left it, not buried in a screenshot folder or lost on a whiteboard that got erased three months ago.

The library carries over from season to season. Plays you built last year are still there this year. And if you're a premium user, you can browse the global library -- a collection of pre-made plays shared by coaches across the community -- and pull anything into your own collection.

How the Play Library Works

  1. Save a Play

    Every play you design in the play designer can be saved to your library with one click. Give it a name and it's stored. You can also favorite plays to pin them to the top so your go-to calls are always visible.

  2. Tag and Organize

    Add situation tags (3rd and short, red zone, two-minute drill) and concept tags (play-action, screen, draw, RPO) to every play. When your library grows past 20 or 30 plays, tags are what make it usable. Filter by any tag and the plays that match surface instantly.

  3. Clone, Share, or Reuse

    Found a play that's been working? Clone it. The copy is ready to edit -- adjust a route, flip the formation, tweak the blocking scheme -- without touching the original. When your staff needs to see the play, share it directly. Players can pull it up on their phones for pre-game review.

Frequently Asked Questions About the Play Library

Is the football play library free?

Yes. Saving plays, tagging, cloning, and sharing are all free. The global library of pre-made community plays is available to premium users.

Do my saved plays carry over between seasons?

Yes. Your play library is permanent. Plays you saved last season, last year, or three years ago are still in your library. Nothing gets deleted unless you remove it.

What's the difference between the play library and a playbook?

The play library is your full collection of every play you've ever saved -- think of it as your filing cabinet. A playbook is a curated, ordered set of plays for a specific team or gameplan. You pull plays from your library into a playbook when you're building a gameplan.

What is the global library?

The global library is a shared collection of plays created by coaches in the RC Football community. Premium users can browse it by formation, concept, or situation and copy any play into their personal library.

Can I share plays with players, not just coaches?

Yes. Shared plays can be viewed by anyone you send them to. Players can open them on their phone or laptop for pre-game review. They see the diagram, the route labels, and any notes you've added.

How does cloning work?

Cloning duplicates a play into a new, independent copy. You can change anything on the clone -- routes, positions, labels, formation -- without affecting the original. Coaches use this to create variations of a base play without starting from scratch each time.
